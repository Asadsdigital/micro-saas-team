# Validation and opportunity selection

## Evidence ledger

For each candidate record buyer, recurring problem, trigger, current workaround, economic impact, alternatives, reachable acquisition channel, source URL/date, evidence type and unknowns. Search complaints, product reviews, pricing pages and customer discussions relevant to that specific job. Respect access restrictions. Do not fabricate interviews or treat search snippets as confirmed product capabilities.

Compare candidates on pain frequency, willingness-to-pay evidence, buyer reachability, differentiation, delivery feasibility, support burden, variable cost and data risk. Use qualitative high/medium/low judgments with reasons unless the user requests scoring. Unknown is not zero and is not a favourable score.

For competitor revenue claims record whether the number means MRR, ARR, lifetime revenue, launch sales or something unspecified. Record who made the claim and whether its verification mechanism was inspected. Revenue does not establish profitability or transferability to WDLON.

## Falsifiable validation

Choose a test that could disprove the main assumption: buyer interviews, a task walkthrough, a manual concierge pilot, a clearly labelled prototype or a landing-page demand test. Draft interview/outreach copy when useful; send it only when authorized.

Define the intended sample, audience qualification, measurement period and proceed/pivot/stop conditions before interpreting results. Example thresholds are proposals, not universal rules. Ask about the last occurrence, actual workaround and spend before asking whether a person likes the idea. Separate compliments and waitlist signups from behavioural commitments and actual payment.

Proceed when the pain recurs, the buyer is reachable, the paid promise is credible and delivery economics are plausible. Pivot when a different segment/job has stronger evidence. Stop or pause investment when evidence contradicts the paid problem or critical access is unavailable. A prototype can remain useful as an explicitly speculative test.

## Economics

Model monthly fixed costs, per-active-customer variable costs, payment fees, support effort and acquisition costs at realistic low/base/high usage. Note currency, tax treatment assumptions, dates and sources. Distinguish cash costs from founder labour.

Contribution per paying customer = net monthly subscription revenue minus variable service/payment/support costs. Operating break-even customers = monthly fixed costs divided by positive contribution, rounded up. If contribution is non-positive, break-even is not achieved by adding customers. Do not invent lifetime value without retention evidence.

Return the recommendation, strongest objection, missing evidence, validation design and estimated cost/time. Do not turn an exploratory request into an unsolicited full product build.
